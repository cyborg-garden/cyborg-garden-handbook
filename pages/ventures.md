# Ventures

The garden pays for itself. Bootstrapping is the first article of the covenant,
so the commercial work is not a separate company bolted on. It is the part of the
same crew that invoices.

Three ventures are running: NimbleCo, Hosaka FM, and DigiCryptids, whose alien
anthropologists study the irrationality of human finance behaviour.

## NimbleCo

[NimbleCo](https://nimbleco.ai/) is the consulting practice. It designs agent
swarms for other organizations, runs them in production, and trains the people
who will still be running them after the engagement ends. That last part is the
difference from most agent consultancies: the deliverable includes operators who
can keep it alive.

Client work is not described here, and will not be. What is public is the
practice itself, the brand assets, and the parts of the stack that got handed
into the commons.

## Hosaka FM

[Hosaka FM](https://hosaka.fm/) answers one question: which musicians are about
to break. It fuses fourteen independent signals, from radio play through festival
bookings down to underground retail, onto a single artist identity across roughly
two million clusters. The live map is open to anyone and needs no signup.

## The open core

Where a venture has a public core, it is a real repository rather than a teaser.
The founder-support work ships its methodology and tooling under MIT, and the
hosted service is the paid part.
