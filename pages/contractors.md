# Working with the garden on contract

Most people who do paid work for the garden are friends doing a short, scoped
job. This is the short version of how that works. Your contract wins over
anything written here.

## You stay independent

Every contract is scoped around deliverables, with a rate and a cap. You are an
independent contractor. You choose how and when you work, you use your own
tools, and you are free to work for anyone else. There is no exclusivity, no set
hours and no on-call duty, and that holds for ongoing monthly work too.

## You can copy from us

The covenant treats copying as the intended use of the work. Take a good move,
sharpen it, and hand it back if you like. Most of the public code is permissively
licensed, and work that proves itself inside the garden moves outward through
[the Alembic](./the-alembic.md) and is released as open source.

## We do a lot of open source

The agent runtime, the console the garden runs on, the research colleague, the
games and the live art are all public repositories. Contract work usually starts
on the inside, and it is built so the move outward is easy: the general shape in
the code, the particulars kept out of it.

## Ask early

There is no such thing as a question too small here. A five-minute question
costs less than a day spent building the wrong thing. The practical details,
from signing to getting paid, arrive with your contract.
